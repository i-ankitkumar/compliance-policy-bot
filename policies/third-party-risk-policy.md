# Third-Party Risk Policy

## Purpose

Sets the minimum due diligence and ongoing oversight required before and
after engaging a vendor, subprocessor, or other third party that will
access, process, or store organizational or customer data.

## Pre-Engagement Due Diligence

Before contracting with a vendor that will handle customer data, the vendor
must complete a security questionnaire covering encryption practices, access
controls, incident history, and subprocessor usage. Vendors handling data
classified as Confidential or above must provide evidence of a current
SOC 2 Type II report, ISO 27001 certification, or equivalent, or undergo an
equivalent internal assessment.

## Contractual Requirements

Contracts with data-processing vendors must include a Data Processing
Agreement (DPA) consistent with applicable data protection law, breach
notification obligations to the organization within 24 hours of the
vendor's own detection, and the right for the organization to audit or
request evidence of compliance on reasonable notice.

## Risk Tiering

Vendors are tiered by the sensitivity of data accessed and the depth of
system integration: Tier 1 (access to customer personal data or production
systems), Tier 2 (access to internal but non-customer data), and Tier 3 (no
data access). Tier 1 vendors receive a full security review; Tier 2 receive
an abbreviated review; Tier 3 receive a lightweight intake check only.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Tier 1 vendor security posture is reassessed annually, or immediately upon
a material change such as a reported breach, ownership change, or expansion
of the vendor's access scope. A current inventory of all active vendors,
their tier, and their data access scope is maintained by the Vendor Risk
team and reviewed quarterly.

## Offboarding

When a vendor relationship ends, all vendor access is revoked and
credentials rotated within 5 business days, and the vendor must provide
written confirmation of data deletion or return in line with the DPA, unless
a longer legal retention obligation applies.
